#+title: Session failures need structured evidence and request scope
#+filetags: :llm-log:issue:expert:

Normalized history and raw captures can contain explicit tool failures, provider
errors, and aborted or token-limited responses without producing expert outcome
evidence. Mining arbitrary transcript prose or treating missing completion fields
as failure would fabricate outcomes. Individual tool and transport observations
also cannot establish the outcome of the whole task or session.

* Implemented resolution
- [[id:llm-log-conservative-session-failure-mining][Mine explicit response evidence through the existing expert owner]]
