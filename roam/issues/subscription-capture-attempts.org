#+title: Subscription routes need safe capture and logical attempt correlation
#+filetags: :llm-log:issue:subscription:

Subscription route aliases alone do not establish rich usage, secret-safe
persistence, trusted local attribution, or safe exhaustion fallback. Quota snapshots
are a separate metadata service, not captured traffic.

Fallback credentials differ from logical inbound headers. An opaque target
credential echoed by a response can bypass pattern-only redaction unless the
capture receives the credentials used by every actual attempt.

[[id:llm-log-safe-subscription-capture][Capture configured subscription traffic with explicit attempt provenance]]
implements the bounded capture slice. Broader stateful protocol conversion and
durable budget/exhaustion coordination remain in #95.
