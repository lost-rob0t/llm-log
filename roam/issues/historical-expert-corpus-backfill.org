#+title: Historical raw captures do not populate newer expert projections
#+filetags: :llm-log:issue:expert:backfill:dataset:

* Symptom
Older =events.jsonl= records remain valid lossless evidence but are absent from expert
projections introduced after those captures were recorded. Directly starting another
expert process to inspect or rebuild the live database would violate the single Tek9
owner boundary.

* Required behavior
- replay historical captures through declared expert operations using stable IDs;
- resume incrementally without silently skipping a failed record;
- persist provider usage without inventing task or pricing context;
- query the live expert through a bounded read-only interface;
- export the complete outcome dataset beyond one bounded query page while retaining
  raw-capture and expert provenance.

* Design
See [[../../research/LLM-LOG-RESEARCH-053-historical-corpus-backfill-query-export.org][LLM-LOG-RESEARCH-053]].

* Resolution
Pending verified implementation on =feature/expert-backfill-query-export=.
