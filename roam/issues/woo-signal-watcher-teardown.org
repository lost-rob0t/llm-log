#+title: Asynchronous Woo teardown races the next event loop
#+filetags: :llm-log:issue:transport:

Sequential native proxy fixtures can abort in libev with "a signal must not be
attached to two different loops". Changing ports does not isolate process-wide
signal watcher ownership. Destroying a Bordeaux thread requests termination but
does not wait for Woo's unwind-protect cleanup to release its signal watchers.

Resolution: [[id:llm-log-synchronous-woo-shutdown][Wait for the Woo thread to finish cleanup before returning from stop-proxy]].
