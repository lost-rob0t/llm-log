#+title: Wait for Woo event-loop cleanup before restarting
#+filetags: :llm-log:solution:transport:

For [[id:llm-log-woo-signal-watcher-teardown][the native fixture teardown race]], stop-proxy requests event-loop thread
termination and joins that thread. The SBCL join accepts the expected aborted
thread result and has a bounded timeout; a still-live thread raises an error.
Woo releases libev signal watchers in its event-loop unwind-protect cleanup.
The public woo:stop only closes its listener and does not finish loop teardown.

The native transport suite includes a semaphore-gated cleanup regression and
repeated real Woo start/request/stop cycles. Runtime verification belongs to the
hosted Nix native config/transport contract.
