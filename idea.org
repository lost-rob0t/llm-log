* Routing and provider resilience

** IDEA OpenRouter exhaustion fallback for depleted sub-provider proxies
:PROPERTIES:
:CREATED: [2026-09-19 Sat]
:ISSUE: #95
:PARENT: #94
:END:
When a configured direct/sub-provider proxy exhausts quota, credits, rate capacity,
or an explicitly classified provider capacity bucket before downstream response
commit, route the logical request through OpenRouter. Preserve strict credential
separation, explicit model mapping, quantization/provider policy, cost attribution,
attempt provenance, circuit breaking, and no replay after streaming begins.
